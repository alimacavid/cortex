## Uncontested subservices in Tri-Cities

Nobody in Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam or Port Moody currently sells these. Each one is deliverable with LIVO's existing crews and equipment, or a small one-time capital purchase.

- **Linen rental program** _(Airbnb · highest value)_ — LIVO owns hotel-grade linen stock and rotates it. Billed every turnover, forever. One-time inventory purchase, recurring margin, and the host is locked in. No competitor in the region does this.
- **Junk + deep clean in one booking** _(Cross-division · unique)_ — Every junk company in Tri-Cities does junk only. Every maid service does cleaning only. Hoarding cleanouts, estate clearances and move-outs all need both, and LIVO is the only company in the market that can invoice one visit for both.
- **Gutter brightening** _(Power washing)_ — Removing black tiger-stripe oxidation from gutter faces. $200–500 elsewhere in Metro Vancouver, zero Tri-Cities supply, and the before/after photo sells itself.
- **Hard water stain removal** _(Power washing)_ — Sprinkler and irrigation mineral etching on glass. High-skill, high-margin, and no local operator advertises it at all.
- **Solar panel cleaning** _(Power washing)_ — Deionised water, no detergent, no warranty risk. Growing installed base across Burke Mountain new builds and effectively no local supply.
- **Named cleanout packages** _(Junk removal)_ — Garage, storage locker, estate, hoarding. Every competitor sells abstract truck fractions. Customers cannot picture half a truck but they can picture their garage.
- **Shed & deck teardown** _(Junk removal)_ — Demolition plus haul in one visit. The highest-value job available without a permit, and no junk hauler in Tri-Cities offers it.
- **Strata bulk pickup day** _(Junk removal)_ — A truck stationed on site for a booked window. One sale, one mobilization, a full truck, and a standing quarterly contract.
- **Laundry & fold, priced** _(Interior)_ — Everyone lists laundry as a possible task. Nobody prices it. It runs in the background while the crew cleans.
- **Landlord turnover package** _(Interior)_ — Deep clean, appliances, wall spot-wash and a dated photo report for the landlord's file. Landlords rebook and never haggle when a photo report is attached.
- **Emergency call-out SLA** _(Airbnb)_ — A published response window for a guest complaint mid-stay. Nobody in the region commits to one, and a host facing a bad review will pay without negotiating.
- **Mid-stay refresh** _(Airbnb)_ — Towel swap and bathroom reset on stays of 7+ nights. Billable revenue between turnovers that currently doesn't exist anywhere in the market.
