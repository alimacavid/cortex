# Cortex - Master R&D / Think Tank

## What Cortex Is

Cortex is the strategy brain of the Landex Group. Private, secret, organized portal for R&D, idea generation, competitor intelligence, pricing strategy, equipment research, and business development across EVERY business in the portfolio (active or pre-active).

## Flow

CORTEX (think, research, strategize) -> master strategy -> Independent Idea Projects in Claude -> execution plans -> Ascentra / Real Operations

## Cortex vs Ascentra

Cortex = R&D think tank (strategy, ideas, research, intelligence)
Ascentra = Operations (customer pipeline, jobs, documents, compliance)
Never mix the two.
