# 08 - Real Estate / Property Investment Strategy

The crown jewel of LandEx Holding.

## The Model
LandEx Holding extra income -> invest in properties -> development building -> rental income -> reinvest -> grow portfolio

## How Each Company Feeds This
- Construction: Renovates acquired properties at internal cost
- Demolition: Preps sites for redevelopment
- Livo: Maintains and turns over properties
- Vorx: Supplies all workers across the portfolio
- STR: Manages short-term rentals on owned/co-hosted properties
- Javids: Professional listing photos and marketing

## STR Inside Real Estate
STR may operate as a division within the real estate entity as long as no additional licensing is triggered.

## Property Types to Target
- Residential rental (long-term tenants)
- Short-term rental (Airbnb, VRBO)
- Multi-unit residential (duplexes, townhomes)
- Development projects (buy, renovate, hold or flip)
