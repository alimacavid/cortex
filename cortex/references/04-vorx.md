# 04 - Vorx

Role: Supply all workers to every subsidiary.

## What Vorx Does
People infrastructure for the entire Landex Group. Feeds workers to every business internally AND operates as an external revenue-generating staffing agency.

## Internal
- Source and screen candidates
- Criminal record and background checks
- Validate licenses and certifications
- Rate and track worker performance
- Maintain live roster of deployable talent

## External Revenue
- Staffing marketplace for trades, construction, general labor
- Temp and permanent placements
- Payroll and compliance (employer-of-record)
- Digital-first, mobile app experience

## App Concept (TO DEVELOP)
- Worker profiles with ratings, certifications, availability
- Employer job posting and instant matching
- Background check integration
- Real-time scheduling and dispatch
- Payment and invoicing
- Performance tracking

## Competitors
- Randstad Canada (national, 60+ offices)
- Tradesmen International (national trades focus)
- Local small agencies
