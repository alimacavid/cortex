# 05 - STR / Real Estate Company

NEEDS A NAME (minimal, memorable, beautiful).
May be part of a broader real estate company that owns properties for rent.

Role: Manage short-term rentals.

## Core Services
- Airbnb co-hosting and co-listing
- Guest management (communication, check-in/out)
- Turnover coordination (Livo handles execution)
- Dynamic pricing optimization
- Listing photography (Javids Pictures)

## Licensing (BC) - Confirmed
- BCFSA license NOT REQUIRED for STR co-hosting
- Provincial STR Registry MANDATORY
- Municipal business license REQUIRED per city
- Principal Residence Rule limits scaling

## STR Inside Real Estate
May operate as a division within the real estate entity as long as no additional licensing is triggered.

## Revenue Model
- Commission: 15-25% of booking revenue
- Add-on services: turnover, photography
- Scale: 10+ properties for consistent cash flow
