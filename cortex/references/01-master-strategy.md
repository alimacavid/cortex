# 01 - Master Strategy

## Landex Group - Strategic Architecture

Parent: Landex Group (Javids Holding)
Brand: Luxury, royal, timeless. Quiet authority, refined elegance. Black, ivory, muted gold. Owner of premium ventures, not an operator.

## The Real Estate End-Goal

Every subsidiary exists to enable rental property acquisition:
Cash Generation -> Property Acquisition -> Internal Renovation -> Managed Rentals -> Portfolio Growth

## Phased Growth Plan

### Phase 1 - Foundation (Current)
- Get LandEx Construction operational (highest margin, most direct enabler)
- Landex Demolition has Ascentra pipeline
- Livo Services being researched in Cortex

### Phase 2 - First Property
- Acquire first rental property
- Renovate using Construction (internal cost savings)
- STR company manages it (Airbnb co-hosting)

### Phase 3 - Scale
- Grow portfolio with Livo + Vorx supporting operations
- Vorx feeds workers to all subsidiaries internally
- STR handles short-term, Livo handles long-term property care

### Phase 4 - Brand at Scale
- Javids Pictures for professional marketing
- Cariva adds care services vertical
- Portfolio generates passive income + appreciation

## Group Synergies

Construction: Feeds renovation to all properties. Gets labor from Vorx, site prep from Demolition.
Demolition: Site clearing, hazmat. Gets labor from Vorx. Feeds cleared sites to Construction.
Livo: Property maintenance, turnovers. Gets workers from Vorx, turnover jobs from STR.
Vorx: Workers to EVERY subsidiary. Gets demand from all businesses.
STR: Rental income, guest management. Gets cleaning from Livo, photos from Javids.
Javids: Marketing, brand content. Gets clients from all businesses.
Cariva: Care revenue, recurring income. Gets caregivers/drivers from Vorx.

## Priority Stack
1. Construction - revenue engine + renovation capability
2. Demolition - already operational via Ascentra
3. STR Company - direct rental income, needs naming
4. Livo - property services, supports everything
5. Vorx - people infrastructure, app development
6. Javids Pictures - brand layer at scale
7. Cariva - future expansion, licensing-heavy
