The specific thing to beat

Capstone publishes $8–15 per window and $180–320 for a full home. Shine City publishes flat rates across every surface. Stop selling the photo report as an add-on

Maidless and Pixel both include before-and-after photos free on every single turnover, and Turno bakes photo updates into the app. Charging for it will read as nickel-and-diming to any host who has shopped around. Include it, say loudly that it's included, and monetise the things nobody else has — linen stock, off-site laundry, mid-stay, and a guaranteed emergency response window.
